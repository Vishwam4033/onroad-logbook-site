OnRoad Logbook

A simple kilometre and toll logbook for rideshare drivers in Australia.

What the app does

  • Records your work trips using your iPhone's GPS.
  • Lets you enter your odometer reading at the start and end of each trip.
  • Asks you when you pass a Queensland toll gantry, so you can keep a record of your tolls.
  • Shows your trip history on a calendar and a map.
  • Lets you export your logbook as a CSV file to keep for your tax records.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to, you can sign in with your Google account and back up your logbook to your own Google Drive. The app only works with the files it creates. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Your privacy

Your trip data stays on your iPhone and in your own Google Drive. The app does not send your data to any other server. Read the full Privacy Policy.
