Privacy Policy

OnRoad Logbook. Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information OnRoad Logbook ("the app") uses, why it uses it, and where it is kept. In short: your data stays on your iPhone and in your own Google Drive. We do not collect it, sell it or share it.

1. Information the app uses

  • Location. While you are recording a trip, the app uses your iPhone's GPS to measure the distance you drive, draw your route on a map, and detect when you pass a toll gantry.
  • Trip details you enter. Odometer readings, trip dates and times, toll records and any notes you add.
  • Google account (optional). If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app uses your Google sign-in to save and load your logbook files in your own Google Drive.

2. Where your information is stored

  • All trip data is stored on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy of your logbook (as CSV files) is saved in your own Google Drive account.
  • The app does not have its own server. We never receive your trips, location or Google data.

3. Google user data

If you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use Google Drive with the drive.file scope. This only lets the app see and change files that the app itself created. It cannot read, change or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

The app uses this access only to back up and restore your logbook. Google user data is not sent to us, not shared with anyone else, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine learning models.

OnRoad Logbook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. You decide if you share an exported logbook file, for example with your accountant.

5. Your choices

  • You can turn off location access at any time in iPhone Settings. Trip recording by GPS will stop, but you can still enter trips by hand.
  • You can sign out of Google in the app at any time.
  • You can remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete your data by deleting the app from your iPhone, and deleting the OnRoad Logbook files from your Google Drive.

6. Children

The app is made for adult rideshare drivers and is not meant for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
